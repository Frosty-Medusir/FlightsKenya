Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Flights Kenya helps you compare Kenyan domestic fares and, if you choose, receive fare-deal alerts. This policy explains how Kodovozlabs handles personal data when you use the service.

We aim to handle personal data in accordance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019) and applicable regulations, including the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021.

1. Who is responsible

Kodovozlabs operates Flights Kenya and is responsible for personal data processed through the service. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@kodovozlabs.co.ke.

2. Information we handle

  • Push alert subscription: if you enable alerts, your browser provides a push endpoint and encryption keys. We store these technical details to deliver fare notifications to that browser. They are not your name or email address, but may be personal data because they are linked to your device or browser.
  • Fare and booking information: configured fare sources provide route, carrier, price, departure, and booking-link information, which we store to compare fares and link to providers.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include in your message.
  • Rate-limiting information: when you subscribe to alerts, our API stores a one-way hash of the request IP address to limit repeated subscription attempts. Older rate-limit records are cleaned up during later subscription requests.
  • Technical information: our website, API, or hosting providers may process connection details such as IP address, browser information, request time, and diagnostic logs to operate and secure the service. The exact logs and retention periods depend on the provider and its configuration.

Flights Kenya does not require an account. Route and fare information is not intended to identify you. We do not sell personal data or use advertising trackers.

3. Why we use information

  • To display fare comparisons and calculate route price history.
  • To send fare alerts when you have chosen to enable browser push notifications.
  • To reply to support requests and maintain service security.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable.

We rely on your consent for push alerts, which you can withdraw at any time. We use operational information to provide and protect the service, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in maintaining a secure and reliable fare-comparison service.

4. Cookies and device storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you install the progressive web app or enable alerts, your browser stores the service-worker cache and push subscription information needed for those features. You can clear browser data or remove the app in your browser or device settings.

5. When information is shared

We share only what is needed to operate Flights Kenya with service providers. Vercel hosts the public website and API and may process technical request or diagnostic logs. GitHub Actions runs the scheduled Python fare collector and may process configured feed URLs and workflow logs. Neon hosts the PostgreSQL database, which stores fare observations, booking links, push subscription details, and hashed rate-limit records. The browser push-notification service selected by your device processes the information needed to deliver alerts. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. We do not sell your personal data.

Some providers may process information outside Kenya. Where that happens, applicable safeguards and transfer requirements under Kenyan data-protection law apply.

6. How long information is kept

  • Push subscription details are kept while alerts remain enabled. Use Disable alerts on the home page to withdraw consent and remove that device's subscription from our application. Revoking browser permission alone may not immediately remove the server-side record; contact us if you need help removing it.
  • Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to respond and handle any follow-up or legal requirements.
  • Technical logs are retained according to the relevant provider's configuration and retention policy.

Fare observations are used to produce historical route comparisons. They are not intended to identify individual users. Our collector deletes observations older than 180 days.

7. Your data-protection rights

Subject to the law and any applicable exceptions, you may ask us for information about your personal data, request access or correction, object to processing, or request deletion. You may withdraw consent for push alerts at any time using the control on the home page. Contact us at info@kodovozlabs.co.ke to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

You may also raise a concern with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

8. Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its revised date.
